Compounding is usually explained with money, but I think it is bigger than that. Knowledge compounds. Taste compounds. Career capital compounds. The ability to make slightly better decisions under uncertainty compounds too.

This piece will be about why small advantages, repeated consistently, can become meaningful over a long enough time horizon.
