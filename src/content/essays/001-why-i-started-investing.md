I did not start investing because I wanted to trade charts or chase quick wins. I started because I became fascinated by the idea that ordinary people can own small pieces of extraordinary businesses.

A good business can compound quietly for years. If you are patient enough to understand it, write down why you own it, and hold through uncertainty, time starts doing a lot of the work for you.

This essay will be the starting point for the site: why I care about investing, what compounding changed in how I think, and why writing my thesis down makes me more honest.
