# Uber (UBER)

These are my personal draft notes for the Uber pitch. The aim is to turn the raw thesis into a clearer article covering the business, valuation, upside, risks, and what I learned.

## Valuation

In 2026, 12 Bn in FCF, 8 Bn in adj. FCF

2024 FCF of 6.5 Bn growing at 40% CAGR

Includes $2bn of SBC (which should not grow) and $2bn (which will grow) of Insurance reserves

Current market cap is ~160 Bn (5% 2026 FCF yield) and expected growth of mid teens at the minimum going forward. Current revenue CAGRs are 20+%

Total 2026 yield is likely >20% on current prices

Management has full control over achieving 2026 targets - driven by margin expansion

For a platform like Uber, you would place atleast a 30x multiple on adj. FCF in 2026 that gets you to ~$250 Bn market cap. This is 50% growth in 2 years on a tralining basis. Share price target of 115 in 2026 atleast

## Three key questions

## Does Uber have runway for growth? YES

They are getting increasingly dominant in US. The Uber one subscription is the best value among delivery services by far

Most competitors are unprofitable and will find it increasingly difficult to continue competing against Uber

Macro tailwinds as car ownership prices continue to increase and Uber provides increasingly better value

May not be in the 20%+ range but mid teens digits is reasonable

They keep experimenting - in US, you can use a courier service, reserve rental cars, get groceries (basically everything) on top of eats (with ads) and mobility. Also partnered with taxis in certain locations.

The power of the platform is underestimated

## Will management be shareholder friendly? Tentative YES

CEO comes expedia, a more mature company

CFO appears shareholder focused

Pulled out of autonomous driving startup in 2020 and are making smaller partnership investments that are better than large transformative acquisitions. Management is clear in recent interviews that autonomous tech will be a commodity and they will focus on providing value through their platform. Makes a ton of sense to me

Still questions over very high SBC (management guided using 6 Bn of buybacks to offset SBC so sharecount should be stable or declining going forward); random equity stakes (management guided to reducing these over time); and clever reporting (which i suppose comes with territory of venture backed companies)

CEO owns 4+ million stock (and options) as of 2024; up from about 1.4 mm in 2022. He has subsequently sold well over 1 mm shares, out of which, atleast 750k were options close to expiration. We will learn more in 2025 proxy soon

## With the advent of self driving, will UBER become redundant. Is there a binary risk? NO

Most tech becomes commodotized sooner rather than later - news about sue of synthetic data to train self driving

Transition will be slow because many many vehicles are already on road

Multimodal world with robots for neighbourhood deliveries in cities (SF pilot) and drones for suburbs (China already cracked this) will all need to place to find demand. And UBER is the funnel for all delivery related demand
