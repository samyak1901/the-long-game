# MercadoLibre (MELI)

MercadoLibre is a study in regional execution: marketplace scale, payments, credit, logistics, and advertising reinforcing each other in markets where local knowledge matters.

## Key business lines

**E-commerce (Marketplace & Mercado Shops)**

- 18 countries, >46M unique buyers
- FX-neutral GMV growth 35% in Q4 2022, led by Brazil & Mexico
- Logistics advantage via Mercado Envios (1B+ items shipped in 2022; 76% delivered <48h)

**Fintech (Mercado Pago & Mercado Credito)**

- 44M active users, TPV $36B in 2022 (+80% YoY)
- Credit & lending solutions for underbanked & merchants
- Brazil PIX adoption drives digital payments growth
- Loyalty programs & installment offerings deepen engagement

**Advertising (Mercado Ads)**

- Monetizes traffic on marketplace → higher conversion for merchants
- Offers precise targeting and measurable ROI

**Logistics & Fulfillment (Mercado Envios Full)**

- Enables expansion into large/heavy items, groceries, furniture
- Potential B2B fulfillment service for 3rd parties → new revenue stream

## Investment thesis

- **LatAm dominance:** Largest e-commerce + fintech ecosystem; difficult for Amazon/Shopee to replicate regional logistics, payments, and cultural knowledge.
- **Network flywheel:** Marketplace → Payment → Credit → Ads → Loyalty. Each service feeds engagement, GMV, and monetization.
- **Fintech upside:** ~45% unbanked population; digital payments adoption growing at 25%+ CAGR.
- **Logistics moat:** Owns fulfillment + shipping network; can expand beyond own platform.
- **Growth optionality:** New categories (grocery, furniture), cross-border expansion, lending scale.

## Risks

- **Regulatory / FX:** Latin American markets volatile; inflation, currency controls (esp. Argentina) could disrupt cash flows.
- **Competition:** Amazon, Shopee (exited some markets), Nubank, PicPay, Rappi. Could pressure margins and customer share.
- **Credit risk:** NPLs increasing; provision for doubtful accounts up 147% in 2022. Slow loan originations mitigate risk.
- **Capital intensity:** Logistics expansion and fintech scale require continuous investment.

## Why now?

- Market is overly focused on the Amazon vs. Shopee narrative; MELI's local dominance and ecosystem effects are underappreciated.
- Shopee's exit in LatAm created a market-share vacuum; MELI is poised to capture it.
- Fintech adoption accelerating due to regulatory changes (PIX in Brazil) and the unbanked population (~45%).
- Logistics and fulfillment scale ensures faster delivery, increasing loyalty and repeat GMV.

## Some signals

- Founder Marcos Galperin retains ~7.5% stake; top institutions include Baillie Gifford (12%), Morgan Stanley, Capital Group.
- Demonstrated long-term commitment; management highly trusted locally.
