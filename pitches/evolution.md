# Evolution AB (EVVTY)

Evolution is a high-margin business with real structural advantages, but also a reminder that regulation, geography, and operational shocks can matter as much as headline margins.

## Valuation

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Market cap | $15.95B |
| EV | $15.2B |
| Shares | 206.56M |
| NTM P/E | 12.7x |
| EV/FCF | 12.3x (~8% FCF yield) |

## What they do

- Live Casino games provider, HQ in Stockholm, founded 2006.
- Market leader: ~60% share of the Live Casino segment.
- Famous for interactive game shows like Crazy Time (tens of thousands play at once).
- B2B model: sells games to casinos/operators, not directly to players → avoids B2C headaches.

## Market & growth

- Global online gambling: €107B in 2023, 7–12% CAGR to 2030.
- Live Casino: +24% CAGR (2019–2023). RNG (slots etc.): +21% CAGR.
- Land-based is still 75% of the total market → huge runway for online.

**Key regions**

- **Asia (39%):** Philippines studio, legalizing markets.
- **Europe (38%):** regulated but fragmented; UK / DE / FR key.
- **US (12.5%):** state-level licenses, 6 studios.
- **LatAm (7%):** Brazil & Colombia expansion 2025.

## Business model

- **Customers:** Operators (online casinos, brick-and-mortar, aggregators).
- **Revenue:** 10–12% commission on operators' wins.
- **Advantages of B2B:** no marketing cost, no payment risks, no age-verification headaches.
- **Margins:** mid-50s to low-60s %; very high for the industry.

## Recent challenges

**Growth slowdown**

- Q4 2024: flat revenue growth in Asia.
- Georgia studio strike impacted ~45–55% of profits.

**Cyber attacks in Asia**

- Streaming manipulated → revenue loss.
- Management working on mitigation.

**Unregulated markets**

- 60% revenue from grey/black markets → risk if regulation tightens.
- Upside: first in line for licenses once legalized.

## Financial snapshot

- 3Y / 5Y CAGR: Revenue +25% / +41%, Net Income +27% / +53%, FCF +30% / +51%.
- Strong balance sheet: €800M cash, no long-term debt.
- Capital allocation: 50% dividend payout, opportunistic buybacks (€500M planned 2025).

## Moat / competitive advantage

- **Scale:** best games, faster rollout, cheaper infrastructure.
- **Regulatory head start:** first licensed in new regions → easier customer capture.
- **Customer stickiness:** top 5 customers = 46% revenue; largest = 13%.
- **Competition:** Playtech, Pragmatic Play, VIVO → smaller scale, lower margins.

## Risks

- Dependence on top customers.
- Regulatory & legal risk in grey/black markets.
- Cybersecurity in Asia.
- Labor issues in key studios (Georgia).

## Upside

- Online gambling still small vs land-based → huge market opportunity.
- Live Casino + game shows → high-growth segment.
- New studios: Philippines, Brazil, US expansion.
- Buybacks at low prices could enhance shareholder returns.
- Buybacks + dividend yield combined around 7%. With mid-single-digit growth we are looking at a 15% return.
- By 2028: 1.5B in earnings at a 15x multiple is a 22.5B market cap (current is ~16B), aligning with buyback + dividend returns.
