# PayPal (PYPL)

PayPal is a turnaround question: whether a scaled, profitable payments business can refocus on branded checkout, Venmo, margins, and capital returns after years of poor narrative.

## Valuation

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Steady-state net income | $4 – $5 Bn |
| Implied multiple today | ~13x on forward 2025 earnings ($65 Bn market cap / ~$5.1 Bn 2025E net income) |
| Historical average multiple | 20 – 25x |
| Share buybacks | 11.7% reduction 2021–2024. YTD '25: 6–7% buybacks done, worth ~$6B |
| Implied valuation (2028) | $121 Bn (6.1–6.2B NE or $7.5 EPS); $7.5 EPS × 20 = ~$140 share price |
| Upside (2028) | ~100% |

## Key growth drivers

- **Branded experiences:** TPV +8%; online checkout TPV +5%
- **Venmo:** TPV +12% (3-year high); revenue +20%
- **Payment Service Provider (Braintree):** Stabilizing and returning to growth
- **Next-gen initiatives:** AI, crypto, ads

## Investment thesis

- **Turnaround underway:** Raised 2025 guidance for TM$ and EPS; growth re-accelerating.
- **Capital returns:** ~$6B share buyback in 2025 (≈7% share-count reduction).
- **Undervalued:** Trading at only 13x forward earnings despite scale (438M users) and profitability.
- **Re-rating potential:** Expanding margins + renewed growth; multiple expansion also likely.

## Target (2028)

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| EPS growth | $5.23 (2025E) → $7.5 (2028E) |
| Target P/E | 20x |
| Target price | $140 |
| Implied net income | ~$6B |

## Key risks

- **Competition:** From tech giants, card networks, and fintechs.
- **Execution:** Success of AI, Ads, PayPal World uncertain.
- **Macro:** Weaker consumer spending could impact volumes.

## Why this opportunity exists

PayPal was mismanaged under prior leadership, chasing super-app features and low-margin features to attract low-end customers, like crypto, savings, interest, cashbacks, etc.

Now, new management is refocused on core strengths: profitable growth, branded checkout, Venmo monetization, and cost efficiency. The core business is healthy and growing, but overall results are still masked by old, low-margin segments being phased out.

## Assumptions

- Incremental take rates from here on are positive.
- Management meets its target for 2027 and beyond.
- Multiple re-rating to 19–20x.
- Margins don't decline from anticipated interest-rate cuts.
