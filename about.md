# About

I'm Samyak Jain. **The Long Game** is where I write about investing, the businesses I find interesting, and the occasional idea borrowed from football.

I started this site for one reason: writing forces me to be honest. It's easy to hold a vague opinion about a company; it's much harder to write down *why* I own it, what would prove me wrong, and whether the thesis still holds a year later. Putting it in public keeps me accountable to my past self.

## What you'll find here

- **Essays**: longer pieces on compounding, ownership, patience, and the mental models I keep coming back to.
- **Stock pitches**: research notes on individual companies, written like a notebook: business, valuation, thesis, risks, and why now.
- **Football**: because the game is one of the clearest live examples of patterns under pressure, and it teaches the same lessons as investing: small edges, timing, and patience.

## How I think about it

I'm a long-term investor, not a trader. I care more about understanding a business deeply than about being right this quarter. The notes here are deliberately unpolished in places, the structure makes them readable while the thinking stays in motion.

## A standing disclaimer

Nothing here is financial advice. These are personal notes about how I think about businesses, risk, and learning. I may own, have owned, or change my mind about any company mentioned.
